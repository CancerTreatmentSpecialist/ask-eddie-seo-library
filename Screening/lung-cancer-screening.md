# Lung Cancer Screening
Lung cancer screening detects cancer early, when treatment is most effective. Screening is recommended for people at higher risk due to smoking history or age.

## Why Lung Screening Matters
- Lung cancer is often diagnosed late.
- Low-dose CT (LDCT) can detect cancer early.
- Early detection significantly improves survival.

## Who Should Be Screened?
### High-Risk Individuals
Annual low-dose CT scan if:
- age 50–80
- AND 20+ pack-year smoking history
- AND currently smoke or quit within the last 15 years

### Not Recommended For
- people with no smoking history
- people under 50
- people with major health issues limiting treatment

## Screening Tool
### Low-Dose CT (LDCT)
- Quick, painless scan.
- Uses lower radiation than standard CT.
- Detects small nodules early.

## Common Findings
### Benign Nodules
Most nodules are not cancer.

### Suspicious Nodules
Require follow-up imaging or biopsy.

### Lung-RADS Categories
- 1–2: benign
- 3: probably benign; short-term follow-up
- 4: suspicious; further testing needed

## Screening Barriers
- transportation barriers
- financial barriers
- provider bias
- fear of radiation
- stigma around smoking
- limited access in rural areas
