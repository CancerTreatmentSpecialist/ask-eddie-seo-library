# Liver Cancer Screening (For Cirrhosis Patients)
Liver cancer screening is recommended for people with cirrhosis or chronic liver disease. Screening detects cancer early, when treatment is more effective.

## Why Liver Screening Matters
- Cirrhosis significantly increases liver cancer risk.
- Early detection improves survival.
- Screening is simple and non-invasive.

## Who Should Be Screened?
### High-Risk Individuals
Screen every 6 months if:
- cirrhosis (any cause)
- chronic hepatitis B (even without cirrhosis)
- chronic hepatitis C with advanced fibrosis

## Screening Tools
### Ultrasound (Every 6 Months)
Primary screening tool for liver cancer.

### AFP Blood Test
Alpha-fetoprotein; used alongside ultrasound.

### MRI or CT
Used when ultrasound is unclear or suspicious.

## Common Findings
### Benign
- cysts
- hemangiomas
- fatty liver changes

### Suspicious
- solid liver masses
- rising AFP levels

## Screening Barriers
- transportation barriers
- financial barriers
- provider bias
- limited access to ultrasound in rural areas
- stigma around liver disease
