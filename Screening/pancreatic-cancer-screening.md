# Pancreatic Cancer Screening (High-Risk Only)
Pancreatic cancer screening is NOT recommended for the general population. It is reserved for people with strong genetic or family risk.

## Why Pancreatic Screening Matters
- Pancreatic cancer is often diagnosed late.
- High-risk individuals benefit from early detection.
- Screening requires specialized imaging.

## Who Should Be Screened?
### High-Risk Individuals Only
Screening considered if:
- BRCA1/2, PALB2, CDKN2A, STK11, Lynch syndrome
- two or more first-degree relatives with pancreatic cancer
- known hereditary pancreatic cancer syndromes

### Not Recommended For
- average-risk individuals
- people without genetic or family risk

## Screening Tools
### MRI/MRCP
Non-invasive imaging of pancreas and ducts.

### Endoscopic Ultrasound (EUS)
Highly sensitive; used in specialized clinics.

### Genetic Counseling
Essential for determining eligibility.

## Common Findings
### Benign
- cysts
- inflammation
- fatty changes

### Suspicious
- solid masses
- concerning cyst features

## Screening Barriers
- limited access to specialized clinics
- financial barriers
- provider bias
- fear of invasive procedures
