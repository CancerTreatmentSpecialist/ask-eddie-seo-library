Financial Barriers in Cancer Care

Reviewed by Dr. Derrick J. Beech, MD, FACS, Surgical Oncologist
Last updated: September 2026

Financial barriers limit access to screening, diagnosis, treatment, and survivorship support. These barriers affect patients across all communities but are especially severe for marginalized groups. They include lack of insurance or underinsurance, high out-of-pocket costs, lost wages during treatment, travel and accommodation expenses, medication costs, and childcare or eldercare needs. These pressures can delay care or force patients to choose less effective treatments.

Impact on Cancer Outcomes

  • Delayed diagnosis
  • Lower treatment completion
  • Reduced access to advanced therapies
  • Higher emergency visits
  • Lower survivorship support
  • Increased mortality

Communities Most Affected

  • Indigenous peoples
  • Black communities
  • Rural residents
  • Low-income households
  • Immigrants and newcomers

High-Impact Cancer Areas

  • Breast cancer — delayed follow-up imaging
  • Colorectal cancer — lower colonoscopy rates
  • Lung cancer — limited access to targeted therapies
  • Prostate cancer — cost barriers to advanced imaging

Evidence-Based Solutions

  • Financial navigation programs
  • Insurance support and benefits counseling
  • Travel and accommodation subsidies
  • Community-based screening
  • Telehealth expansion
  • Employer support programs

How AskEddy Helps
