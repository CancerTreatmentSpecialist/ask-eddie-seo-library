Transportation Barriers in Cancer Care

Transportation challenges are one of the most common reasons patients miss screening, delay diagnosis, or struggle to complete treatment — especially in rural, remote, and low‑income communities.

---

Overview

Transportation barriers include:

• long distances to cancer centers
• limited public transit
• weather and seasonal road conditions
• cost of fuel, parking, or accommodation
• lack of reliable vehicles
• mobility challenges


These barriers disproportionately affect Indigenous, rural, and low‑income patients.

---

Impact on Cancer Outcomes

• Missed screening appointments
• Delayed follow-up after abnormal results
• Interrupted treatment schedules
• Increased emergency presentations
• Lower survivorship support


---

Communities Most Affected

• Rural and remote communities
• Indigenous Nations
• Low-income households
• Seniors
• Patients without family support


---

High‑Impact Cancer Areas

• Colorectal cancer — missed colonoscopies
• Breast cancer — delayed mammograms
• Lung cancer — limited access to CT screening
• Cervical cancer — missed Pap/HPV tests


---

Evidence‑Based Solutions

• Mobile screening units
• Travel vouchers and mileage reimbursement
• Community driver programs
• Tele-oncology
• Local navigation support
• Partnerships with Indigenous and rural health organizations
