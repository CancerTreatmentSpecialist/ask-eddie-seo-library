# Cancer-Related Fatigue
Fatigue is one of the most common and challenging side effects of cancer treatment. It feels different from normal tiredness and can affect daily life.

## What Fatigue Feels Like
- extreme tiredness that doesn’t improve with rest
- heavy limbs or slowed movement
- difficulty concentrating
- lack of motivation
- feeling “wiped out” after small tasks

## Common Causes
- chemotherapy
- radiation therapy
- immunotherapy
- anemia
- sleep disruption
- stress or anxiety
- poor nutrition

## When Fatigue Is Concerning
- sudden worsening
- fatigue with shortness of breath
- fatigue with dizziness or palpitations
- fatigue interfering with daily activities
