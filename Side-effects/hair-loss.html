Side Effects

Hair Loss (Alopecia)

Reviewed by Dr. Derrick J. Beech, MD, FACS, Surgical Oncologist
Last updated: September 2026

Hair loss is a common side effect of chemotherapy and some targeted therapies. It can affect scalp hair, eyebrows, eyelashes, and body hair.

What Hair Loss Looks Like

  • Thinning hair
  • Patchy loss
  • Complete loss
  • Sensitive scalp
  • Changes in texture when hair regrows

Common Causes

  • Chemotherapy — see Chemotherapy
  • Targeted therapy (certain drugs)
  • Radiation to the head

When Hair Loss Is Concerning

  • Painful scalp
  • Redness or infection
  • Sudden patchy loss without treatment

Emotional Impact

Hair loss can affect self-esteem, identity, and privacy, since it's a visible sign of treatment.

How AskEddy Helps

AskEddy supports patients by explaining hair loss patterns, clarifying what to expect, preparing questions for clinicians, and reducing fear and confusion.
