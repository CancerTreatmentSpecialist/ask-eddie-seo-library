# Nausea and Vomiting
Nausea and vomiting are common side effects of chemotherapy, radiation, and certain medications. They can range from mild queasiness to severe vomiting.

## What Nausea Feels Like
- queasiness
- stomach discomfort
- loss of appetite
- sensitivity to smells
- vomiting or dry heaving

## Common Causes
- chemotherapy
- radiation to the abdomen
- pain medications
- anxiety
- dehydration

## When Symptoms Are Concerning
- vomiting more than 3–4 times a day
- inability to keep fluids down
- dizziness or fainting
- dark urine or no urine
- vomiting with blood
