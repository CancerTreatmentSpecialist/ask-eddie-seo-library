Side Effects

Skin Changes

Reviewed by Dr. Derrick J. Beech, MD, FACS, Surgical Oncologist
Last updated: September 2026

Skin changes are common during cancer treatment. They may include dryness, redness, itching, peeling, or sensitivity.

Common Skin Symptoms

  • Dryness or cracking
  • Redness or rash
  • Itching
  • Peeling
  • Sensitivity to sunlight
  • Darkening of skin
  • Nail changes

Common Causes

  • Radiation therapy
  • Chemotherapy — see Chemotherapy
  • Immunotherapy
  • Targeted therapy

When Symptoms Are Concerning

Blistering, open sores, or fever with skin changes need prompt medical attention.
  • Severe rash
  • Blistering
  • Open sores
  • Fever with skin changes
  • Painful skin reactions

How AskEddy Helps

AskEddy supports patients by explaining skin changes, clarifying when follow-up is needed, preparing questions for clinicians, and reducing fear and confusion.
