# Blood Count Changes
Cancer treatments can affect blood counts, leading to anemia, low white blood cells, or low platelets. These changes can cause fatigue, infections, or bleeding.

## Types of Blood Count Changes
### Anemia (Low Red Blood Cells)
- fatigue
- shortness of breath
- dizziness

### Neutropenia (Low White Blood Cells)
- higher infection risk
- fever
- chills

### Thrombocytopenia (Low Platelets)
- easy bruising
- bleeding
- nosebleeds

## Common Causes
- chemotherapy
- radiation therapy
- certain targeted therapies

## When Symptoms Are Concerning
- fever (always requires medical evaluation)
- bleeding that doesn’t stop
- severe fatigue
- shortness of breath
