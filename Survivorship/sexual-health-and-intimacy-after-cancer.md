# Sexual Health and Intimacy After Cancer
Cancer treatment can affect sexual health, intimacy, and body image. These changes are common and often under-discussed.

## Common Physical Changes
### Women
- vaginal dryness
- pain during intercourse
- hormonal changes
- decreased libido

### Men
- erectile changes
- decreased libido
- hormonal changes
- pelvic discomfort

## Emotional Changes
- body image concerns
- fear of intimacy
- anxiety or depression
- relationship stress

## Communication Matters
- open conversations with partners
- discussing concerns with clinicians
- exploring new forms of intimacy

## When Symptoms Are Concerning
- persistent pain
- severe dryness
- erectile changes lasting months
- emotional distress affecting relationships
