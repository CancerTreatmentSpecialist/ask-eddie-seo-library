# Follow-Up Care and Surveillance
After treatment ends, regular follow-up helps detect recurrence early, manage long-term effects, and support recovery. Surveillance plans vary by cancer type.

## Why Follow-Up Matters
- monitors for recurrence
- manages late effects
- supports emotional well-being
- provides ongoing guidance

## Typical Follow-Up Schedule
### First 2–3 Years
- visits every 3–6 months
- imaging or lab tests as needed

### Years 3–5
- visits every 6–12 months

### After 5 Years
- annual visits
- screening based on age and risk

## Common Follow-Up Tests
- physical exams
- blood tests
- imaging (CT, MRI, ultrasound)
- tumor markers (if relevant)
- survivorship care plans

## What Patients Often Ask
- “How often will I need scans?”
- “What symptoms should I watch for?”
- “Who coordinates my care now?”
- “When do I return to regular screening?”
