Fear of Recurrence

Reviewed by Dr. Derrick J. Beech, MD, FACS, Surgical Oncologist
Last updated: September 2026

Fear of cancer returning is one of the most common and persistent feelings after treatment. It is normal, understandable, and shared by nearly all survivors.

What Fear of Recurrence Feels Like

  • Worry before follow-up scans
  • Anxiety about new symptoms
  • Fear triggered by news or reminders
  • Difficulty planning for the future
  • Intrusive thoughts

Why It Happens

  • Trauma from diagnosis and treatment
  • Uncertainty about the future
  • Physical reminders (scars, fatigue)
  • Ongoing medical appointments

When Fear Is Concerning

  • Constant worry
  • Panic attacks
  • Avoidance of medical care
  • Sleep disruption
  • Difficulty functioning

Strategies That Help

  • Grounding techniques
  • Predictable follow-up schedule
  • Talking with care team
  • Connecting with other survivors
  • Counseling or support groups

How AskEddy Helps

AskEddy supports patients by explaining recurrence risk, clarifying follow-up plans, preparing questions for clinicians, and reducing fear and confusion.
