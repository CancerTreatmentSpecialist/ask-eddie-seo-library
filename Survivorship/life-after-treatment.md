
# Life After Cancer Treatment
Finishing cancer treatment is a major milestone, but the transition into survivorship can feel overwhelming. Many patients experience mixed emotions, physical changes, and uncertainty about what comes next.

## Common Feelings After Treatment
- relief and gratitude
- fear of recurrence
- anxiety about follow-up scans
- uncertainty about the future
- pressure to “get back to normal”
- emotional exhaustion

## Physical Changes
- fatigue
- changes in appetite
- weight changes
- lingering pain or stiffness
- sleep disruption

## Emotional Changes
- anxiety
- depression
- grief for lost time or abilities
- difficulty adjusting to a new normal

## Building a New Routine
- gradual return to activity
- balanced nutrition
- gentle exercise
- sleep hygiene
- reconnecting with social supports
