# Late Effects of Cancer Treatment
Late effects are health changes that appear months or years after treatment. They vary by treatment type and can affect physical, emotional, or cognitive health.

## Common Late Effects
### Physical
- fatigue
- neuropathy
- heart or lung changes
- hormonal changes
- infertility
- chronic pain

### Emotional
- anxiety
- depression
- PTSD-like symptoms
- fear of recurrence

### Cognitive (“Chemo Brain”)
- memory issues
- difficulty concentrating
- slower processing

## Why Late Effects Occur
- chemotherapy
- radiation therapy
- surgery
- immunotherapy
- targeted therapy

## When Symptoms Are Concerning
- worsening over time
- interfering with daily life
- new symptoms years after treatment
- symptoms with weight loss or fatigue
