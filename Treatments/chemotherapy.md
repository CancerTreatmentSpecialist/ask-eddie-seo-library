# Chemotherapy
Chemotherapy uses drugs to kill cancer cells or stop them from dividing. It can be given alone or combined with other treatments.

## Why Chemotherapy Matters
- treats cancer throughout the body
- shrinks tumors before surgery
- kills remaining cells after surgery
- improves survival in many cancers

## How Chemotherapy Is Given
- IV infusion
- oral pills
- injections
- continuous pumps

## Common Uses
- breast cancer
- colorectal cancer
- lung cancer
- lymphoma
- ovarian cancer

## What Patients Often Ask
- “Will I lose my hair?”
- “How long does chemo last?”
- “What side effects should I expect?”
- “Can I keep working?”

## Side Effects (General)
- fatigue
- nausea
- hair loss
- low blood counts
- neuropathy
