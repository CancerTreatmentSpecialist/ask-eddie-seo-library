# Radiation Therapy
Radiation therapy uses high-energy beams to destroy cancer cells. It can be used alone or combined with surgery, chemotherapy, or other treatments.

## Why Radiation Matters
- kills cancer cells
- shrinks tumors
- relieves symptoms
- reduces recurrence after surgery

## Types of Radiation
### External Beam Radiation
Most common; delivered from a machine.

### IMRT / IGRT
Advanced forms that shape radiation precisely.

### Stereotactic Radiation (SBRT/SRS)
High-dose, highly targeted treatment.

### Brachytherapy
Radiation placed inside the body near the tumor.

## Common Uses
- breast cancer
- prostate cancer
- lung cancer
- head and neck cancers
- brain tumors
