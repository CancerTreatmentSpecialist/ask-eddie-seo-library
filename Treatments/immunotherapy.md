# Immunotherapy
Immunotherapy helps the immune system recognize and attack cancer. It is one of the most important advances in modern cancer care.

## Why Immunotherapy Matters
- boosts immune response
- targets cancer cells more precisely
- can lead to long-lasting remission
- works when other treatments fail

## Types of Immunotherapy
### Checkpoint Inhibitors
Block proteins that stop the immune system (e.g., PD-1, PD-L1, CTLA-4).

### CAR-T Therapy
Engineered immune cells that attack cancer.

### Monoclonal Antibodies
Target specific proteins on cancer cells.

### Cancer Vaccines
Help the immune system recognize cancer.

## Common Uses
- lung cancer
- melanoma
- kidney cancer
- lymphoma
- MSI-H cancers
