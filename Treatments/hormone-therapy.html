Hormone Therapy

Reviewed by Dr. Derrick J. Beech, MD, FACS, Surgical Oncologist
Last updated: September 2026

Hormone therapy blocks or lowers hormones that fuel certain cancers. It is commonly used in breast and prostate cancer.

Why Hormone Therapy Matters

  • Slows or stops cancer growth
  • Reduces recurrence risk
  • Often taken for years
  • Can be used with other treatments

Common Uses

Breast Cancer

ER/PR-positive cancers — see Tumor Markers for how these are identified.

Prostate Cancer

Androgen-driven cancers.

Types of Hormone Therapy

Breast

  • Tamoxifen
  • Aromatase inhibitors

Prostate

  • Androgen deprivation therapy (ADT)
  • Anti-androgens

What Patients Often Ask

  • "How long will I take hormone therapy?"
  • "What side effects should I expect?"
  • "Will this replace chemo?"
  • "Why do hormones matter?"
