# Hormone Therapy
Hormone therapy blocks or lowers hormones that fuel certain cancers. It is commonly used in breast and prostate cancer.

## Why Hormone Therapy Matters
- slows or stops cancer growth
- reduces recurrence risk
- often taken for years
- can be used with other treatments

## Common Uses
### Breast Cancer
ER/PR-positive cancers.

### Prostate Cancer
Androgen-driven cancers.

## Types of Hormone Therapy
### Breast
- tamoxifen
- aromatase inhibitors

### Prostate
- androgen deprivation therapy (ADT)
- anti-androgens
