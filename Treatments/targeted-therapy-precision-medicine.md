# Targeted Therapy
Targeted therapy uses drugs that attack specific mutations or proteins in cancer cells. It is more precise than chemotherapy.

## Why Targeted Therapy Matters
- focuses on cancer-specific changes
- often has fewer side effects
- can be taken as pills
- works well for mutation-driven cancers

## Common Targets
- EGFR (lung cancer)
- HER2 (breast cancer)
- BRAF (melanoma)
- KRAS (colorectal cancer)
- ALK/ROS1 (lung cancer)
- PIK3CA (breast cancer)

## How Targeted Therapy Is Given
- oral pills
- IV infusions

## What Patients Often Ask
- “Do I need genetic testing?”
- “What mutation do I have?”
- “Will targeted therapy replace chemo?”
- “How long will I take it?”
