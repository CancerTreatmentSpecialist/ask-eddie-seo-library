# Cervical Symptoms
Cervical symptoms often relate to infections or hormonal changes, but persistent or unusual symptoms may require evaluation. HPV is the main cause of cervical cancer.

## Common Cervical Symptoms
- abnormal vaginal bleeding
- bleeding after intercourse
- pelvic pain
- pain during intercourse
- unusual discharge
- lower back pain

## Abnormal Bleeding
Includes:
- bleeding between periods
- heavier periods
- bleeding after menopause

## Bleeding After Intercourse
Often caused by:
- infection
- inflammation
- cervical changes
Persistent bleeding requires follow-up.

## Pelvic Pain
Concerning when:
- persistent
- not related to menstrual cycle
- occurs with bleeding or discharge

## Unusual Discharge
May be:
- watery
- bloody
- foul-smelling
Persistent discharge requires evaluation.

## Concerning Features
- symptoms lasting weeks
- symptoms worsening over time
- symptoms with weight loss or fatigue
- symptoms with abnormal bleeding
