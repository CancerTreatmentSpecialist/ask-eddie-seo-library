# Pancreatic Symptoms
Pancreatic symptoms often appear late and may overlap with other digestive conditions. Persistent or worsening symptoms require medical evaluation.

## Common Pancreatic Symptoms
- upper abdominal pain
- back pain
- jaundice
- unexplained weight loss
- loss of appetite
- nausea or vomiting
- new-onset diabetes

## Abdominal and Back Pain
Pain may:
- radiate to the back
- worsen after eating
- improve when leaning forward

## Jaundice
Occurs when the bile duct is blocked.

## Weight Loss and Appetite Changes
Often related to:
- digestive enzyme issues
- metabolic changes

## New-Onset Diabetes
May be an early sign in some cases.

## Concerning Features
- persistent pain
- jaundice with itching
- rapid weight loss
- nausea that does not improve
- symptoms with fatigue
