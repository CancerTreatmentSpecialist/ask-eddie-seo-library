Prostate Symptoms

Reviewed by Dr. Derrick J. Beech, MD, FACS, Surgical Oncologist
Last updated: September 2026

Prostate symptoms are common and often caused by benign enlargement or inflammation. Certain symptoms may require evaluation to rule out cancer or other conditions.

Common Prostate Symptoms

  • Weak urine stream
  • Difficulty starting urination
  • Frequent urination (especially at night)
  • Urgency
  • Pelvic discomfort
  • Blood in urine or semen
  • Erectile changes

Urinary Symptoms

Often caused by benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH), infection, or inflammation. Persistent symptoms may require evaluation.

Weak Stream or Hesitancy

Occurs when the prostate presses on the urethra.

Frequent Urination

Especially at night (nocturia).

Urgency

Sudden need to urinate.

Pelvic Discomfort

May feel like pressure, heaviness, or a dull ache.

Blood in Urine or Semen

This requires medical evaluation.

Erectile Changes

Can be related to blood flow, nerves, or prostate conditions.

Concerning Features

  • Symptoms worsening over time
  • Symptoms with weight loss or fatigue
  • Blood in urine or semen
  • Persistent pelvic pain
