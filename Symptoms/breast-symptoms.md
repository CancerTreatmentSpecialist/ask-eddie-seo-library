# Breast Symptoms
Breast symptoms are common and often benign, but certain changes require medical evaluation. Understanding these symptoms helps patients know when to seek care.

## Common Breast Symptoms
- new lump or mass
- breast pain
- nipple discharge
- skin dimpling or puckering
- changes in breast size or shape
- redness or swelling
- inverted nipple (new onset)

## What Lumps Usually Mean
Most lumps are benign:
- cysts
- fibroadenomas
- infections
- hormonal changes

## Concerning Features
- hard, fixed lump
- lump that grows over time
- skin changes (orange peel texture)
- bloody nipple discharge
- persistent pain in one area

## Nipple Changes
- discharge (clear, milky, or bloody)
- inversion
- crusting or scaling

## Skin Changes
- redness
- thickening
- rash that does not improve
