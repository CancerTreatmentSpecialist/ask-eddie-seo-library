
# Colorectal Symptoms
Colorectal symptoms can be caused by benign conditions, but persistent or worsening symptoms require medical evaluation.

## Common Colorectal Symptoms
- blood in stool
- change in bowel habits
- abdominal pain or cramping
- unexplained weight loss
- fatigue
- feeling of incomplete bowel movement

## Blood in Stool
### Bright Red Blood
Often from hemorrhoids or fissures.

### Dark or Tarry Stool
May indicate bleeding higher in the colon.

## Changes in Bowel Habits
- diarrhea
- constipation
- narrower stools
- alternating patterns

## Abdominal Symptoms
- bloating
- cramping
- discomfort that does not improve

## Concerning Features
- persistent symptoms
- symptoms lasting more than a few weeks
- symptoms with weight loss
- symptoms with anemia or fatigue
