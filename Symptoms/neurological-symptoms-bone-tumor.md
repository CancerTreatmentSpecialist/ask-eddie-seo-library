# Neurologic Symptoms (Brain Tumors)
Neurologic symptoms can be caused by migraines, infections, or nerve conditions. Certain symptoms may indicate pressure or changes within the brain.

## Common Neurologic Symptoms
- persistent headaches
- seizures
- vision changes
- weakness or numbness
- balance problems
- speech difficulties
- personality or behavior changes

## Headaches
Concerning when:
- persistent
- worsening over time
- worse in the morning
- accompanied by nausea or vomiting

## Seizures
New-onset seizures require urgent evaluation.

## Vision Changes
- blurred vision
- double vision
- loss of peripheral vision

## Weakness or Numbness
May affect:
- face
- arms
- legs
- one side of the body

## Behavior or Cognitive Changes
- memory issues
- confusion
- personality shifts

## Concerning Features
- symptoms progressing over weeks
- symptoms with vomiting
- seizures
- sudden weakness
- severe headaches with vision changes
