# Skin Symptoms
Skin symptoms are common and often benign, but certain changes may require medical evaluation. Persistent or evolving skin changes should be assessed by a healthcare provider.

## Common Skin Symptoms
- new or changing moles
- lumps or bumps
- sores that do not heal
- itching or burning
- bleeding or crusting lesions
- changes in color or texture

## Concerning Features
### Melanoma Warning Signs (ABCDE)
- Asymmetry
- Border irregularity
- Color variation
- Diameter > 6 mm
- Evolving (changing)

### Non-Melanoma Skin Cancer Signs
- pearly or shiny bumps
- scaly patches
- sores that bleed or don’t heal
- firm red nodules

## Benign Skin Findings
- moles
- skin tags
- seborrheic keratoses
- cysts

## When Symptoms Are Concerning
- rapid changes
- bleeding without injury
- persistent sores
- new pain or itching
- changes in long-standing moles
