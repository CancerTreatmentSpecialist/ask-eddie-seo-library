Pancreatic Symptoms

Reviewed by Dr. Derrick J. Beech, MD, FACS, Surgical Oncologist
Last updated: September 2026

Pancreatic symptoms often appear late and may overlap with other digestive conditions. Persistent or worsening symptoms require medical evaluation.

Common Pancreatic Symptoms

  • Upper abdominal pain
  • Back pain
  • Jaundice
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Loss of appetite
  • Nausea or vomiting
  • New-onset diabetes

Abdominal and Back Pain

Pain may radiate to the back, worsen after eating, or improve when leaning forward.

Jaundice

Occurs when the bile duct is blocked.

Weight Loss and Appetite Changes

Often related to digestive enzyme issues or metabolic changes.

New-Onset Diabetes

May be an early sign in some cases.

Concerning Features

Jaundice with itching, rapid weight loss, or persistent pain with fatigue warrant prompt evaluation.
  • Persistent pain
  • Jaundice with itching
  • Rapid weight loss
  • Nausea that does not improve
  • Symptoms with fatigue
