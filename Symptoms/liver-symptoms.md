# Liver Symptoms
Liver symptoms often relate to inflammation, infection, or chronic liver disease. Certain symptoms may indicate more serious conditions and require evaluation.

## Common Liver Symptoms
- abdominal swelling (ascites)
- jaundice (yellowing of skin or eyes)
- right upper abdominal pain
- nausea or loss of appetite
- unexplained weight loss
- fatigue
- dark urine or pale stools

## Jaundice
Occurs when bilirubin builds up. Causes include:
- hepatitis
- bile duct blockage
- liver cancer (less common but important)

## Abdominal Swelling (Ascites)
May indicate:
- cirrhosis
- liver failure
- advanced liver disease

## Right Upper Abdominal Pain
Can be related to:
- gallbladder disease
- liver inflammation
- liver masses

## Concerning Features
- symptoms lasting weeks
- symptoms worsening over time
- jaundice with abdominal pain
- weight loss or fatigue
- swelling with shortness of breath
