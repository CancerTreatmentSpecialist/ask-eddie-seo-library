# Lung Symptoms
Lung symptoms can be caused by infections, inflammation, smoking-related conditions, or cancer. Persistent or worsening symptoms should be evaluated by a healthcare professional.

## Common Lung Symptoms
- chronic cough
- coughing up blood
- shortness of breath
- chest pain
- wheezing
- hoarseness
- recurrent respiratory infections

## Chronic Cough
A cough lasting more than 8 weeks may be caused by:
- smoking
- asthma
- infection
- reflux
- lung cancer (less common but important)

## Coughing Blood (Hemoptysis)
Even small amounts require medical evaluation.

## Shortness of Breath
May be related to:
- lung disease
- heart conditions
- anemia
- lung cancer (especially if progressive)

## Chest Pain
Concerning when:
- persistent
- worsens with breathing
- occurs with cough or weight loss

## Hoarseness
Can occur when nerves near the lungs are affected.

## Concerning Features
- symptoms lasting weeks
- symptoms worsening over time
- symptoms with weight loss or fatigue
- repeated infections in the same area
