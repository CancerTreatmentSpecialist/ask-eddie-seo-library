Lung Symptoms

Reviewed by Dr. Derrick J. Beech, MD, FACS, Surgical Oncologist
Last updated: September 2026

Lung symptoms can be caused by infections, inflammation, smoking-related conditions, or cancer. Persistent or worsening symptoms should be evaluated by a healthcare professional.

Common Lung Symptoms

  • Chronic cough
  • Coughing up blood
  • Shortness of breath
  • Chest pain
  • Wheezing
  • Hoarseness
  • Recurrent respiratory infections

Chronic Cough

A cough lasting more than 8 weeks may be caused by smoking, asthma, infection, reflux, or lung cancer (less common but important).

Coughing Blood (Hemoptysis)

Even small amounts of blood when coughing require medical evaluation.

Shortness of Breath

May be related to lung disease, heart conditions, anemia, or lung cancer (especially if progressive).

Chest Pain

Concerning when persistent, when it worsens with breathing, or when it occurs with cough or weight loss.

Hoarseness

Can occur when nerves near the lungs are affected.

Concerning Features

  • Symptoms lasting weeks
  • Symptoms worsening over time
  • Symptoms with weight loss or fatigue
  • Repeated infections in the same area
