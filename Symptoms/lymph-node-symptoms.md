
# Lymph Node Symptoms
Lymph nodes often swell due to infection, inflammation, or immune response. Certain changes may require evaluation to rule out more serious conditions.

## Common Lymph Node Symptoms
- swelling or enlargement
- tenderness or pain
- firmness or hardness
- lumps in neck, armpit, or groin
- persistent swelling without infection

## Benign Causes
- viral infections
- bacterial infections
- skin inflammation
- recent vaccination

## Concerning Features
- nodes that are hard or fixed
- nodes larger than 2 cm
- swelling lasting more than 3–4 weeks
- nodes that continue to grow
- nodes with weight loss or night sweats

## Locations to Monitor
- neck
- armpits
- groin
- above collarbone (more concerning)
