Breast Symptoms

Reviewed by Dr. Derrick J. Beech, MD, FACS, Surgical Oncologist
Last updated: September 2026

Breast symptoms are common and often benign, but certain changes require medical evaluation. Understanding these symptoms helps patients know when to seek care.

Common Breast Symptoms

  • New lump or mass
  • Breast pain
  • Nipple discharge
  • Skin dimpling or puckering
  • Changes in breast size or shape
  • Redness or swelling
  • Inverted nipple (new onset)

What Lumps Usually Mean

Most lumps are benign: cysts, fibroadenomas, infections, or hormonal changes.

Concerning Features

A hard, fixed lump; a lump that grows over time; orange-peel skin texture; or bloody nipple discharge should be evaluated promptly.
  • Hard, fixed lump
  • Lump that grows over time
  • Skin changes (orange peel texture)
  • Bloody nipple discharge
  • Persistent pain in one area

Nipple Changes

Discharge (clear, milky, or bloody), inversion, or crusting or scaling.

Skin Changes

Redness, thickening, or a rash that does not improve.

How AskEddy Helps
