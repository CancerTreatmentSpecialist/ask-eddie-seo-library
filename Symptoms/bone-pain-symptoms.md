
# Bone Pain Symptoms
Bone pain can result from injury, arthritis, or inflammation. Persistent or unexplained bone pain may require evaluation.

## Common Bone Pain Symptoms
- deep, aching pain
- pain that worsens at night
- swelling or tenderness
- reduced mobility
- pain without injury

## Benign Causes
- muscle strain
- arthritis
- overuse
- vitamin deficiencies

## Concerning Features
- pain lasting weeks
- pain worsening over time
- pain at night or at rest
- swelling or lumps
- pain with weight loss or fatigue

## Fracture-Like Symptoms
- sudden severe pain
- inability to bear weight
- deformity or swelling
