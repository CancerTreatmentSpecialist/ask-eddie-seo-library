Colorectal Symptoms

Reviewed by Dr. Derrick J. Beech, MD, FACS, Surgical Oncologist
Last updated: September 2026

Colorectal symptoms can be caused by benign conditions, but persistent or worsening symptoms require medical evaluation.

Common Colorectal Symptoms

  • Blood in stool
  • Change in bowel habits
  • Abdominal pain or cramping
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Fatigue
  • Feeling of incomplete bowel movement

Blood in Stool

Bright Red Blood

Often from hemorrhoids or fissures.

Dark or Tarry Stool

May indicate bleeding higher in the colon.

Changes in Bowel Habits

Diarrhea, constipation, narrower stools, or alternating patterns.

Abdominal Symptoms

Bloating, cramping, or discomfort that does not improve.

Concerning Features

Persistent symptoms lasting more than a few weeks, especially with weight loss or anemia and fatigue, warrant evaluation.
