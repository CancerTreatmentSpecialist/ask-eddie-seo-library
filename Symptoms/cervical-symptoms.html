Cervical Symptoms

Reviewed by Dr. Derrick J. Beech, MD, FACS, Surgical Oncologist
Last updated: September 2026

Cervical symptoms often relate to infections or hormonal changes, but persistent or unusual symptoms may require evaluation. HPV is the main cause of cervical cancer.

Common Cervical Symptoms

  • Abnormal vaginal bleeding
  • Bleeding after intercourse
  • Pelvic pain
  • Pain during intercourse
  • Unusual discharge
  • Lower back pain

Abnormal Bleeding

Includes bleeding between periods, heavier periods, or bleeding after menopause.

Bleeding After Intercourse

Often caused by infection, inflammation, or cervical changes. Persistent bleeding requires follow-up.

Pelvic Pain

Concerning when persistent, not related to menstrual cycle, or occurring with bleeding or discharge.

Unusual Discharge

May be watery, bloody, or foul-smelling. Persistent discharge requires evaluation.

Concerning Features

Symptoms lasting weeks, worsening over time, or occurring with weight loss, fatigue, or abnormal bleeding warrant evaluation.
