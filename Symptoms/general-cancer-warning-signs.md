# General Cancer Warning Signs
Cancer symptoms vary widely, but certain warning signs should never be ignored. These symptoms do not mean cancer is present, but they signal the need for medical evaluation.

## Common Warning Signs
- unexplained weight loss
- persistent fatigue
- new lumps or masses
- changes in bowel habits
- persistent cough or hoarseness
- abnormal bleeding
- difficulty swallowing
- new or changing skin lesions
- persistent pain without clear cause

## System-Specific Symptoms
### Breast
- new lump
- nipple discharge
- skin dimpling

### Colorectal
- blood in stool
- change in bowel habits
- abdominal pain

### Lung
- chronic cough
- coughing blood
- shortness of breath

### Cervical
- abnormal bleeding
- pelvic pain
- pain during intercourse

### Prostate
- weak urine stream
- frequent urination
- pelvic discomfort

## When Symptoms Are Concerning
Symptoms are more concerning when:
- they persist for weeks
- they worsen over time
- they interfere with daily life
- they occur with weight loss or fatigue
