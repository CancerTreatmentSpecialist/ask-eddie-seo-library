Neurological Symptoms & Bone Tumors

Reviewed by Dr. Derrick J. Beech, MD, FACS, Surgical Oncologist
Last updated: September 2026

Neurologic symptoms can be caused by migraines, infections, or nerve conditions. Certain symptoms may indicate pressure or changes within the brain.

Common Neurologic Symptoms

  • Persistent headaches
  • Seizures
  • Vision changes
  • Weakness or numbness
  • Balance problems
  • Speech difficulties
  • Personality or behavior changes

Headaches

Concerning when persistent, worsening over time, worse in the morning, or accompanied by nausea or vomiting.

Seizures

New-onset seizures require urgent evaluation.

Vision Changes

Blurred vision, double vision, or loss of peripheral vision.

Weakness or Numbness

May affect the face, arms, legs, or one side of the body.

Behavior or Cognitive Changes

Memory issues, confusion, or personality shifts.

Concerning Features

  • Symptoms progressing over weeks
  • Symptoms with vomiting
  • Seizures
  • Sudden weakness
  • Severe headaches with vision changes
