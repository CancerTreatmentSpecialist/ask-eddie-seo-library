Bone Pain Symptoms

Reviewed by Dr. Derrick J. Beech, MD, FACS, Surgical Oncologist
Last updated: September 2026

Bone pain can result from injury, arthritis, or inflammation. Persistent or unexplained bone pain may require evaluation.

Common Bone Pain Symptoms

  • Deep, aching pain
  • Pain that worsens at night
  • Swelling or tenderness
  • Reduced mobility
  • Pain without injury

Benign Causes

  • Muscle strain
  • Arthritis
  • Overuse
  • Vitamin deficiencies

Concerning Features

Sudden severe pain, inability to bear weight, or visible deformity or swelling needs prompt medical attention — these can indicate a fracture.
  • Pain lasting weeks
  • Pain worsening over time
  • Pain at night or at rest
  • Swelling or lumps
  • Pain with weight loss or fatigue
